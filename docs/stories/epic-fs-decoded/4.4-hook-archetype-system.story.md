## Description

As the ShadowFeed content engine, I need 6 hook archetypes implemented with weighted random selection and anti-repeat logic, so that every generated post starts with a proven scroll-stopping hook pattern from the @brandsdecoded__ playbook instead of random LLM-generated hooks.

The 6 archetypes come directly from Research §06 of the Brand Spy Report. Each archetype has a structure template with variable slots, pillar affinities, weighted probability, and concrete examples.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — Define 6 Hook Archetypes (AC: 1)

Create `src/modules/forge-shadowfeed/shadowfeed-hook-archetypes.ts`:

| # | Archetype | Structure Template | Weight | Best For Pillars |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `direct-controversy` | "[Fato contraintuitivo] que [grupo] não quer que você saiba" | 0.15 | wake-up-slap, educational-value |
| 2 | `specific-number` | "[X] coisas que [consequência negativa]" | 0.25 | educational-value, brand-breakdown |
| 3 | `transformative-promise` | "Como [pessoa] fez [resultado impossível] em [tempo curto]" | 0.15 | proof-social, brand-breakdown |
| 4 | `polarization` | "[Declaração que divide opiniões]" | 0.15 | wake-up-slap |
| 5 | `curiosity-mystery` | "O motivo real pelo qual [X] não funciona" | 0.15 | educational-value, wake-up-slap |
| 6 | `brand-comparison` | "[Marca A] vs [Marca B]" | 0.15 | brand-breakdown |

Each archetype includes:
- `id`: HookArchetype type value
- `structureTemplate`: String with `{variable}` slots
- `examples`: 3 concrete examples (from Research §06)
- `bestForPillars`: PillarId[] affinity list
- `weight`: Selection probability (all sum to 1.0)
- `description`: What makes this archetype effective

- [x] Implemented

### Task 2 — Implement Weighted Selection with Anti-Repeat (AC: 2)

```typescript
function selectHookArchetype(
  pillarId: PillarId,
  recentArchetypes: HookArchetype[] // last 2 used
): HookArchetype
```

- Filter archetypes by pillar affinity (boost weight for `bestForPillars` match)
- Exclude archetypes used in last 2 consecutive posts (anti-repeat)
- Weighted random selection from remaining pool
- Query `sf_shadowfeed_queue` for last 2 posts to get `recentArchetypes`

- [x] Implemented

## Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weight distribution may need tuning after real-world testing | High | Low — sub-optimal hook variety, not a blocker | Weights are configurable constants; adjust post-launch based on engagement metrics |
| Anti-repeat with only 6 archetypes may over-constrain selection | Low | Low — with last-2 exclusion, 4 archetypes always remain | 6 archetypes with last-2 exclusion leaves minimum 4 options; sufficient variety |
| `//` visual signature prefix may not render well in all contexts | Low | Low — cosmetic only | Apply at ~30% probability (configurable); easy to disable if problematic |
| LLM ignores archetype structure template and generates freestyle hooks | Medium | Medium — defeats the purpose of archetypes | Reinforce with FR-HOOK-07 declarative rule + include concrete example in prompt Block 5 |
