## Description

As the ShadowFeed content engine, I need every generated post to track its position in the 70/20/10 conversion pyramid (funnel_tier) and every non-offer post to contain a natural product seed via the double-door system, so that even free-value content subtly promotes ShadowFeed — replicating the @brandsdecoded__ strategy where "every free post is simultaneously content and product showcase."

The conversion pyramid distributes content across three tiers: top (70% — free value), middle (20% — proof/social), bottom (10% — direct offer). The double-door system maps each content theme to a specific product seed with varying intensity (whisper/mention/spotlight).

## Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product seeds feel forced/unnatural in generated captions | Medium | HIGH — audience perceives posts as ads, reduces trust | Use "whisper" intensity for top-of-funnel (subtle); provide 2-3 seed variants per pillar for rotation |
| LLM does not integrate seed text smoothly into copy | Medium | Medium — seed appears bolted-on rather than woven in | Include seed instruction in prompt Block 6 with explicit placement guidance; test with few-shot examples |
| 90% seed presence target may be aggressive | Low | Low — metric miss, not functional failure | Track actual rate; adjust prompt reinforcement if below 80% |
| Offer pillar anchoring prices become outdated | Medium | Low — stale pricing in generated content | Store current prices in `shadowfeed-constants.ts`; update when pricing changes |
